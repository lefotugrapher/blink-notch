How should the blinking email look?

Each one drops out of the notch, blinks a few times, then goes back up. They loop, so just watch.

A. Googly chaos

Wobbling envelope with googly eyes. The eyes blink out of sync, and one stays shut a beat too long.

B. Flap slam

One big eyeball inside the envelope. The flap is the eyelid, so it slams shut and pops open again, squashing the whole thing.
